What it does

Most music bots give you a queue and a "now playing" message and stop there. Vibe goes a bit further.

The player

/watch opens it right in your voice channel, and it updates live for everyone watching. Drag the seek bar, click a queued song to jump straight to it, or hide the queue when you just want the artwork.

DJ roles that stick

Set a DJ role and it covers the player too, so nobody gets around it by opening /watch. Non-DJs see the buttons greyed out with a reason, and a skip vote from the player counts toward the same vote as /skip.

Boost what you want next

Everyone in the channel shares one queue, and anyone can boost a song in it from the player. A boost counts as a vote, so nobody has to jump the line alone, and a DJ can send a track straight to next.

For streamers

Turn on the now-playing overlay in /config and Vibe gives you a link to use as an OBS browser source. It shows the title, artist and artwork of what's playing, follows skips and pauses, and only ever shows that. You can replace the link or switch it off whenever you like.

Stats worth checking

Vibe counts how long you listen, your daily streak, your level and your badges. /rank draws you a card, /leaderboard ranks the server, and it all follows you between servers. On your profile page you can pick the colours of your rank card and player, with a live preview.

It doesn't fall over

When a track fails, Vibe quietly tries other sources before giving up. When an audio server dies mid-song, it moves to a spare one and picks up where it was. Usually you won't notice either happened.

Everything else

/like saves whatever's playing. Save whole playlists too, edit the queue with /remove and /move, add filters, pull up lyrics. /help sorts every command by category.

Questions people ask

Is Vibe a free Discord music bot?

Yes. The music is free: what Vibe plays, which services it plays from and how it sounds are the same for everybody, with no ads and nothing locked behind voting.

How do I add Vibe to my server?

Use the Add button above and pick your server. You need the Manage Server permission there, which is Discord's rule. Vibe works the moment it joins, with nothing to configure first.

How do I play music?

Join a voice channel and run /play with a song name, a YouTube or Spotify link, or a playlist link. Everyone in the channel shares one queue.

What is /watch?

A live player that opens inside Discord, in the voice channel: artwork, a seek bar you can drag, and the whole queue, in sync for everyone. Run /watch while you're in a voice channel.

What permissions does Vibe ask for, and can it read my messages?

Five: View Channels, Send Messages, Embed Links, Connect and Speak. It asks for no privileged intents and cannot read your messages.

Can I control who is allowed to skip or pause?

Yes. Set a DJ role with /config and it covers the player as well as the commands. Skipping is a vote for everyone else, and the same vote whichever way it is cast.

Does my listening history follow me between servers?

Yes. Your listening time, streak, level, badges and saved songs belong to you, not to a server. What Vibe stores, and how to see or delete it, is in the privacy policy.
